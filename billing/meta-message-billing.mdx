---
title: "Meta message billing"
description: "Choose whether Meta invoices the WhatsApp Business Account or your wallet pays the published rate."
---

`meta_billing_mode` is set on the setup link and stored for that WhatsApp Business Account. It does not change later on the same account.

| Mode | Wallet | Meta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `customer_managed` | Records `meta_rate`. `total_charge` is zero | Meta invoices the account |
| `partner_managed` | Debits the published rate, plus FX and the external fee when they apply | Meta may still invoice the account until a credit line is attached |

The default is `customer_managed`. If the account already has its own payment method, setup stays customer-managed (`reason` `existing_payment_method`) and sends are not paused.

A partner-managed account cannot be switched back to customer-managed on the same WhatsApp Business Account.

## Pass-through

The wallet charge equals Meta's stored USD rate for that category and region.

- When `waba_currency` is not `USD`, a 5% FX margin is applied.
- When messages are not processed by LazyApp, a further 5% is applied.
- Tax is not added. The payment fee is recorded and is not part of `total_charge`.

## Funding status

`GET /v1/whatsapp/accounts/{wabaId}/funding` (scope `billing:read`) returns the local funding row:

```json
{
  "data": {
    "waba_id": "1234567890",
    "status": "not_funded",
    "reason": "not_attached",
    "meta_billing_mode": "partner_managed",
    "paid_messages": { "paused": false, "reasons": [] }
  }
}
```

Statuses are `funded`, `pending`, `unknown`, `not_funded`, and `revoked`. Until `META_CREDIT_LINE_ID` is set, ensure records `not_attached` and does not call Meta's credit-line API. `funded` is not set automatically.

`POST /v1/whatsapp/accounts/{wabaId}/funding` with `business_portfolio_id` (scope `customers:write`) records that status. A new run that is not funded returns `202`. A repeat within 30 seconds reuses the last operation and returns `200`.

## Usage

`GET /v1/whatsapp/accounts/{wabaId}/usage?start_time=...&end_time=...` and the portfolio equivalent return LazyApp's recorded charges for at most 31 days. Amounts are micro-USD (`total_charge` × 1,000,000). `time_basis` is `recorded_at`. `charge_scope` is `lazyapp_recorded_charges`.

## Stop

`POST /v1/whatsapp/portfolios/{portfolioId}/funding/revoke` with an `Idempotency-Key` (scope `customers:write`) returns `202` and sets funding to `revoked`, pauses paid messages, and records `funding_revoked`. This does not call Meta. A later ensure on a revoked account returns `409`.
