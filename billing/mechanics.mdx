```text
plan allowance  →  counted on each message (not a wallet debit)
partner-managed →  usage row, then wallet debit of total_charge
customer-managed → usage row with meta_rate, total_charge = 20% of that rate
empty wallet or plan cap → HTTP 402
```

## What charges the card

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Subscribe | Checkout for Pro, or an Enterprise arrangement. On success the account plan updates, and included wallet credits are applied when that plan has them |
| Wallet top-up (₹1,000 / ₹2,500 / ₹5,000 / ₹10,000) | Checkout. On `payment.succeeded` the wallet is credited and an invoice is stored |
| Claim onboarding credits | One-time wallet credit (default **₹100**) when a billing account is created. Managed customer workspaces do not receive it |

## When a message is debited

1. LazyApp resolves the Meta rate for the category and recipient region.
2. `customer_managed` stores that rate and sets `total_charge` to 20% of it. Meta invoices the published rate.
3. `partner_managed` sets `total_charge` to the Meta rate plus 20%, then FX and the external fee when they apply.
4. Templates and other non-service sends debit immediately when the wallet is prepaid-active.
5. Live service sends stay `settlement_status` `pending` with a zero total until Meta reports `pricing.billable`. `false` waives the row (`free_reason` `meta_service_allowance`). `true` quotes and debits once.
6. Test mode charges service immediately, at the Meta rate plus 20% when the account is partner-managed, and at 20% of the Meta rate when it is customer-managed. The Meta service allowance and the in-window free rule do not apply.

Failed deliveries stay free unless failed-message charging is enabled. Utility inside the 24-hour window is charged unless that free rule is enabled.

Re-recording the same message does not debit again after the wallet was charged, the row is pending, or the row was waived.

## Platform customers

| Mode | Wallet |
| --- | --- |
| `inherit` | Debits the platform team's wallet for `total_charge` |
| `prepaid` | Debits that customer's wallet |
| `postpaid` | Records the row and debits neither wallet |

Markup stays on the retail figure you invoice. LazyApp does not collect it.

Plan counters (messages, integration calls, connected numbers) use the paying team: the platform parent when the workspace is a platform customer.

## Pauses

Partner-managed templates and other non-service sends pause when the prepaid wallet is at or below zero (`insufficient_credits`). Service pauses with `service_credits_exhausted` only after a settled, non-free service charge exists and the balance is at or below zero. The plan cap pauses with `plan_message_allowance_exhausted` even when the wallet still has funds.

An empty wallet does not, by itself, block every workspace action. The send path checks the precise pause.

## Invoices

Successful provider payments create invoice rows. The console **Billing** page lists invoices and wallet transactions.
