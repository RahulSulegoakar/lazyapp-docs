WhatsApp messaging has **categories** and **regions**. LazyApp records both on the usage ledger and adds 20% on Meta's published rate. A partner-managed account debits the wallet for that total. A customer-managed account records the rate and debits only the 20%.

## Categories

| Category | Typical use | Who usually starts it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Service** | Free-form replies inside the 24-hour [customer service window](/concepts/glossary#customer-service-window) | Contact (then you reply) |
| **Utility** | Order updates, reminders, account notices (approved templates) | Business |
| **Authentication** | OTPs and login codes (approved templates) | Business |
| **Marketing** | Offers, promotions, broadcasts (approved templates) | Business |

Free-form (`type: text`, media, interactive, …) is treated as **service**. Templates use the category on the template (or the `template.category` you send).

## What is free in LazyApp

Configurable in `pricing.free_rules` (defaults):

| Rule | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service inside the customer service window | Off | Live service waits for Meta's `pricing.billable` instead of assuming the window is free |
| Utility inside the window | Off | Utility templates follow the rate table unless you enable the free rule or the rate row marks `free_within_service_window` |
| Monthly free service allowance | **0** | No local monthly service allowance. Live partner-managed service is settled from Meta's billable flag |
| Failed deliveries | Not charged | Failed outbound does not debit unless you enable charging failures |

Test mode charges service immediately and ignores the in-window rule and any monthly allowance. Treat `is_free` and `free_reason` on the usage row as what LazyApp billed.

## India wallet prices

[lazyapp.in/pricing](https://lazyapp.in/pricing) shows what an Indian wallet pays before the 5% FX margin. These figures are Meta's India USD rate plus 20%, converted at ₹96.43 per dollar. They are not a contract. Meta revises rates, and the conversion rate can change.

| Category | Partner-managed | Customer-managed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing | ₹1.31 | ₹0.22 |
| Utility | ₹0.19 | ₹0.03 |
| Authentication | ₹0.15 | ₹0.03 |
| Service | ₹0.36 | ₹0.06 |

Partner-managed is the published rate plus 20%. Customer-managed is only the 20%, because Meta invoices the published rate. Service is charged. It is not free.

When the WhatsApp account currency is not USD, partner-managed adds a further 5%. Messages that do not run through LazyApp add another 5% on that partner total.

For the full country matrix Meta publishes, see [Meta WhatsApp pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing). The ledger stores USD region rates. Check **Usage** in the console for what your workspace actually charged.

## Rate table API

```bash
curl "https://lazyapp.in/v1/pricing/rates?category=marketing&region=IN" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LAZYAPP_API_KEY"
```

Scope `billing:read`. Filter with `category` and `region`. Cursor-paginated.

## Messaging analytics

Thin workspace aggregates (counts by status / category / direction for the last N days, max 90):

```bash
curl "https://lazyapp.in/v1/analytics/messaging?days=7" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LAZYAPP_API_KEY"
```

Scope `messages:read`. Not a social-analytics clone.

## Regions

Recipient country is inferred from the E.164 dialling prefix (longest match). Unknown prefixes fall back to `DEFAULT`. Examples of configured prefixes include `91` → INDIA, `1` → NORTH_AMERICA, `55` → BRAZIL — see `config/pricing.php` in the application for the full map.

## Calls

WhatsApp Calling is a [capability](/concepts/capabilities) on the connection. Business-initiated calling is subject to **Meta's** calling policies and per-minute fees by destination country; Meta blocks business-initiated calls in some countries while inbound may still work.

LazyApp does **not** publish a separate carrier-minute rate card or recording add-on like a CPaaS voice product. Do not assume Zernio-style “carrier leg + Meta leg” invoices from LazyApp. Use Meta's calling documentation for destination fees, and confirm calling is enabled on [`GET /v1/capabilities`](/concepts/capabilities) before you build UX around it.

## Payment method on the WABA

<Warning>
On `customer_managed`, Meta invoices the WhatsApp Business Account and the wallet pays LazyApp’s 20%. Keep a payment method on the account or delivery can stop even when your LazyApp wallet has funds. On `partner_managed`, the wallet pays the published rate plus 20%, and Meta may still invoice the account until a credit line is attached. See [Payment issues](/billing/payment-issues).
</Warning>
