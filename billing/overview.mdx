---
title: "Pricing"
description: "Plan allowances and Meta message fees are two separate bills."
---

LazyApp bills you for the product. Meta bills delivered WhatsApp messages. Those charges never mix.

1. **A plan** sets how many messages, numbers, and integration calls the workspace can use, plus HTTP and workflow limits.
2. **Meta message fees** are either left on the WhatsApp Business Account, or passed through your LazyApp wallet when you choose partner-managed billing.

There is no monthly free-message ladder on the wallet. The plan allowance is the product cap. The wallet pays Meta's published rate only when that WhatsApp Business Account is partner-managed.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Messages / month | Numbers included | Extra number | Media | Integration calls / month | API requests / minute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | 2,000 | 1 | — | 1 GB | 0 | 100 |
| **Pro** | $9 / month | 100,000 | 2 | $5 | 100 GB | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | — | Unlimited | Unlimited | 4,000 |

A null allowance means unlimited. Free workspaces cannot run workflow integration calls. Workflow starts are limited per second: 5 on Free, 15 on Pro, and 30 on Enterprise.

Pro includes two connected numbers. Each number past that is debited at **$5** once a month from the wallet, for as long as the number stays connected. Free does not add a per-number charge.

Enterprise is arranged with support. Checkout uses the product configured for that plan. A new workspace can claim a one-time welcome credit (default **$1**). Managed customer workspaces do not receive it. Sends stop when a monthly allowance is used up. Extra messages are not billed one by one.

The Platform plan is not offered. A workspace already on it keeps that allowance until the plan changes. Older stored codes map to Pro: `starter`, `growth`, and `business`.

## Two bills

| Bill | Who charges | Where it shows up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | LazyApp | Message count, number overage, storage, integration calls |
| Meta message fee | Meta, or your wallet when partner-managed | Usage ledger `meta_rate` and, when partner-managed, `total_charge` |

`customer_managed` is the default. LazyApp records `meta_rate` and debits nothing. Meta may still invoice the WhatsApp Business Account.

`partner_managed` debits the wallet for the published Meta rate on that category and region. A 5% FX margin applies when the account currency is not USD. A further 5% applies when messages are not processed by LazyApp. Tax is not added on the pass-through. The payment fee is stored on the usage row and is not added to `total_charge`.

LazyApp does not attach a Meta credit line until `META_CREDIT_LINE_ID` is configured. Until then, funding status stays `not_attached`. The wallet can still debit the published rate for a partner-managed account, and Meta may still invoice that account.

See [Meta message billing](/billing/meta-message-billing) and [WhatsApp message rates](/billing/whatsapp-rates).

## What stops a send

| Condition | `code` | HTTP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan message allowance used up | `plan_message_allowance_exhausted` | `402` |
| Partner-managed wallet at or below zero | `insufficient_credits` | `402` |
| Partner-managed service after a settled charge and an empty wallet | `service_credits_exhausted` | `402` |

Reactions are free and are not paused, but they count toward the plan allowance. Read receipts do not count. Inbound messages count toward the plan and are not charged.
