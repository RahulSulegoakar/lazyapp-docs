There is no monthly plan. Add money to the workspace wallet and each delivered WhatsApp message comes out of that balance. Connecting a number does not add a fee. There is no message cap.

Recharge packs credit the same rupees you pay:

| Pack | Wallet credit |
| --- | --- |
| ₹999 | ₹999 |
| ₹2,500 | ₹2,500 |
| ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| ₹10,000 | ₹10,000 |

Sending stops when the wallet is empty. LazyApp sends one reminder before the balance runs low. You can turn on automatic recharge.

API speed limits stay in place so a workspace cannot flood the API. They are not a product allowance. Free workspaces are limited to 100 requests a minute and 5 workflow starts a second.

## Agencies

A brand or a platform customer can price on top of LazyApp in one of two ways:

- A percentage on the wallet price.
- A rupee amount added to each message type: marketing, utility, authentication, and service.

Either can also charge an optional monthly platform fee. On a brand, that fee is debited from the customer wallet once a month and the difference is the brand’s earning. On a prepaid platform customer, the customer’s own balance is debited. An inherit customer still debits the platform wallet at LazyApp’s price. The platform invoices any markup itself.

## One wallet

| Charge | Where it shows up |
| --- | --- |
| Delivered message | Usage ledger `meta_rate` and `total_charge`, then the wallet |
| Brand or platform price | The customer wallet, or the platform’s own invoice |
| Platform fee | Once a month, when the agency turned it on |

`customer_managed` is the default. LazyApp records `meta_rate` and debits 20% of it. Meta may still invoice the WhatsApp Business Account for the published rate.

`partner_managed` debits the wallet for the published Meta rate plus 20%, converted from dollars into rupees at the configured rate. A 5% FX margin applies when the account currency is not USD. A further 5% applies when messages are not processed by LazyApp. Tax is not added. The payment fee is stored on the usage row and is not added to `total_charge`.

LazyApp does not attach a Meta credit line until `META_CREDIT_LINE_ID` is configured. Until then, funding status stays `not_attached`. The wallet can still debit a partner-managed account, and Meta may still invoice that account.

See [Meta message billing](/billing/meta-message-billing) and [WhatsApp message rates](/billing/whatsapp-rates).

## What stops a send

| Condition | `code` | HTTP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A configured message limit is used up | `plan_message_allowance_exhausted` | `402` |
| Partner-managed wallet at or below zero | `insufficient_credits` | `402` |
| Partner-managed service after a settled charge and an empty wallet | `service_credits_exhausted` | `402` |

Reactions are free and are not paused. Read receipts do not count. Inbound messages are not charged.
