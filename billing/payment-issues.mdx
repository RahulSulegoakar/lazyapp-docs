---
title: "Payment issues"
description: "What to do when sends stop because of the plan, the wallet, or Meta billing."
---

Sends return `402` for billing. Other refusals stay `422`. Branch on `code`.

| `code` | Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `insufficient_credits` | Partner-managed templates or other non-service sends, and the prepaid wallet is at or below zero | Top up the wallet that this workspace draws (yours, the platform wallet on `inherit`, or the customer wallet on `prepaid`) |
| `service_credits_exhausted` | The wallet is empty and a settled service charge has already been taken | Top up before the next service reply |

The public wallet has no message cap. `plan_message_allowance_exhausted` is returned only when a message cap is configured on the paying account. Reactions and inbound messages count toward that cap. Read receipts do not.

`integration_call_allowance_exhausted` and `media_storage_exhausted` work the same way: they fire only when that account has an integration-call cap or a media storage cap. The public wallet has neither.

## Customer-managed accounts

An empty LazyApp wallet does not pause these sends. Meta still invoices the WhatsApp Business Account, and a funded wallet is debited LazyApp’s 20%. If Meta stops delivery, fix the payment method on the WhatsApp Business Account. Setup that finds an existing payment method stays customer-managed and does not pause sends.

## Partner-managed accounts

The wallet must cover Meta’s rate plus LazyApp’s 20%. Service is not paused on the first empty-wallet check. It pauses after Meta has marked a service message billable and that charge settled.

Funding status `revoked` pauses paid messages (`funding_revoked`). `not_attached` means no credit line is configured. The wallet can still debit, and Meta may still invoice the account.

## Platform customers

`postpaid` records usage and does not debit a wallet, so an empty wallet is not the pause. A cap configured on the parent account still applies.
