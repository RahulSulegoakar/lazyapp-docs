---
title: "Broadcasts"
description: "Template sends to many contacts, with compliance built in."
---

A broadcast sends one approved template to a segment or an explicit list of numbers.

```bash
curl https://lazyapp.in/v1/broadcasts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LAZYAPP_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "August sale",
    "template_name": "seasonal_offer",
    "language": "en",
    "contact_segment_id": 12,
    "schedule_at": "2026-08-20T10:00:00+05:30",
    "quiet_hours": { "start": "21:00", "end": "09:00", "timezone": "Asia/Kolkata" },
    "frequency_cap_days": 7,
    "start": false
  }'
```

Target either `contact_segment_id` or `phone_numbers` (up to 50,000).

## Compliance is enforced at send time, not create time

This is deliberate. A broadcast created on Monday and sent on Friday respects the opt-outs that happened on Wednesday.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Opt-outs" icon="ban">
    Suppressed contacts are excluded. There is no override.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Frequency caps" icon="calendar">
    `frequency_cap_days` skips anyone who already received a marketing message inside that
    window.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quiet hours" icon="moon">
    Sending pauses during quiet hours in the contact's timezone and resumes after.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Messaging limits" icon="gauge">
    Sends are paced to your number's Meta messaging tier rather than being rejected in bulk.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Excluded recipients are recorded with a reason, so you can explain exactly why someone did not receive a campaign.

## Starting and watching

Create with `"start": true`, or start later:

```bash
curl -X POST https://lazyapp.in/v1/broadcasts/{uuid}/start \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LAZYAPP_API_KEY"
```

`GET /v1/broadcasts/{uuid}` returns live `stats`. You also receive `campaign.started`, `campaign.completed` and `campaign.failed` [webhooks](/webhooks/events).

<Note>
  Marketing templates need an opt-in you can evidence. Broadcasting to a purchased list is
  the fastest route to having your number's quality rating cut and your messaging limit
  reduced.
</Note>
