LazyApp is a WhatsApp Business Platform provider. You get one REST API, one set of webhooks, and a console for the people who work in the inbox every day.

You do not talk to Meta directly. LazyApp holds the WhatsApp Business Account, the phone numbers and the credentials, and gives you a stable API in front of them.

## Two things worth knowing up front

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Every key belongs to one workspace and one mode">
    A key is either `live` or `test`. Test keys never reach a real phone. Nothing you
    read or write with a key can reach another workspace, so there is no account id to
    pass on requests. See [Workspaces and modes](/concepts/workspaces-and-modes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some features depend on Meta approving your account">
    Calling, block lists and India payments are gated by Meta per account. Ask
    [`GET /v1/capabilities`](/concepts/capabilities) what a number supports rather than
    assuming — it can change without a deploy on your side.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
